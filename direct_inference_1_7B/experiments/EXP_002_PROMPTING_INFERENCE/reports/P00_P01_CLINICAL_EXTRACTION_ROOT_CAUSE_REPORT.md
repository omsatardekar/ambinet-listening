# P00 / P01 clinical extraction: root-cause report (Qwen3-1.7B, 30 development records)

**DEVELOPMENT-ONLY.** 30 of the 50 prompt-development records, generator-derived labels, previously used for 0.6B prompt development. Nothing here is independent or clinical validation. Evidence base: the record-by-record audit (`P00_P01_RECORD_BY_RECORD_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`) and its data tables in `evaluations/p00_p01_audit/`. All 30 records were audited against their own transcript and gold label (reconciliation: 30/30 matched, no missing, duplicate or mismatched files).

## 1. Where the 1,093 gold facts go (P01, all 30 records)
| Outcome of a gold fact | P00 | P01 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fully correct (key + every checked attribute) | 44 (4.0%) | 63 (5.8%) |
| Matched but with an attribute error (status 14/28, speaker 41/45, detail 14/17, invented route 15/17) | 84 | 107 |
| Present under different wording, not matched by the strict matcher (WRONG_NORM) | 168 | 190 |
| Missed (includes all facts of records whose JSON failed) | 797 | 733 |
| **Present in some form** | 296 (27%) | 360 (33%) |

- Strict TP in the headline (P00 128, P01 170) = fully correct + attribute-flawed matches. Only 63 of P01's 170 "true positives" are fully clean facts.
- Of P01's 431 strict false positives, 190 are really gold facts in other wording, 88 are true statements absent from gold, 30 are recognisably wrong (placeholders or medications written as allergies, facts in the wrong category) and 123 are not supported by the transcript.
- On the 20 records both prompts completed (688 gold facts): present in some form 254 (37%) for P00 vs 269 (39%) for P01; fully correct 39 vs 49.

## 2. What P01 actually changed
Same-record facts on the 20 shared records: 15 facts correct only in P01, 5 only in P00 (lists in the gap analysis).
- **Genuine improvements (formatting-linked):** vital signs fully correct 11 -> 20 and wrong speaker on matched facts 39 -> 24, because P01's rule "roles are words, not ids" was partly obeyed; records with all speakers correct 10/23 -> 17/25; a few past-medical-history facts (1 -> 4 fully correct). Evidence-grounding strict 0.6% -> 1.8%.
- **Regressions or no change:** status errors 13 -> 21 (social history `ongoing` instead of an allowed value 4 -> 10); invented route 13 -> 17 (the rule "route is oral or null" reads as permission); schema violations 504 -> 614; `confidence` still omitted; layout instruction ignored in 30/30 outputs.
- **Clinical content:** symptoms, negatives, actions, ROS, physical examination, conditions, uncertainties: essentially unchanged (section 3). P01's extra score is therefore mostly completion: structural closure of the failed outputs (`INVALID_JSON_TRUNCATION_AND_RECOVERY_ANALYSIS.md`) lifts P00 to micro F1 0.212 and P01 to 0.221, a gap of 0.008 against the 0.040 headline difference. **P01 has not been shown to extract more than P00.**

## 3. Root causes, ranked by measured impact
Type: **Prompt** = fixable by instruction; **Model** = capability limit of a 1.7B model with thinking off; **Eval** = strict-matcher or label convention; **Schema** = output contract; **Evidence** = evidence generation.

## 4. Category notes (P01 unless stated; examples are from real files)
- **Chief complaint:** captured in some form in 17-19 of 20 shared records; a clean match in 1-2. Gold value `00042`: "constant thirst" / normalised "Polydipsia"; both prompts output "constant thirst" for both fields. Problem: gold normalised term vs raw wording, and sentence-length values.
- **Symptoms:** `00042` gold Polydipsia (current), Fatigue, Polydipsia (resolved: "A bit, last year. It settled by itself back then." SEG0011); output: "constant thirst" only. Resolved earlier episode and fatigue both missed.
- **Negatives:** `00042` SEG0012 "Did you get chest pain, vomiting or weight loss?" SEG0013 "None, as far as I know." gold: three negatives; SEG0014-15 "foot sores" denied: a fourth. P00 and P01 output none of the four; no negative findings at all in `00157` (gold 6) either. The few negatives that were produced are the stock three.
- **History:** surgery is the best history category (gallbladder removal present, but not normalised to Cholecystectomy). Social history: gold uses six categories (living 20, occupation 17, exercise 17, diet 16, alcohol 11, smoking 7); P01 writes `lifestyle`, `work`, `eating`, `living alone`, `drinking`... and `ongoing`/`''` status.
- **Medications:** the most reliable category: no fabricated drug names, 0 dose errors on 33 matched doses, 37/72 recalled. Errors are status (`planned`), invented route, `once a day` placed in `dose` (`00264` iron tablets), a missed second prescription (`00157` fluticasone; P01 puts it in an action instead of medications).
- **Allergies:** gold has only 13 allergy facts; the two prompts together wrote 21 recognisably wrong allergy entries (10 + 11: placeholders and medications). `00157`: allergy entry "none" with type environmental, reported by patient. This is an error type with potential patient-safety meaning; the P02 rules are strict.
- **Vitals:** recall depends on the transcript saying "heart rate 78" in a measurement sentence; items are often named by a variant name or missing (`00157`: five vitals, none extracted).
- **Physical examination / ROS / conditions:** zero fully correct. The model writes the doctor's whole sentence ("feet look fine no ulcers or sores and the pulses are palpable") vs gold "No foot ulcers"; ROS lists inferred systems ("diabetes", "gynecological") that the doctor never asked about (17 unsupported ROS entries in P01); conditions are put in PMH only.
- **Assessment / investigations (second strongest):** assessment: 15 of 48 fully correct and 8 more present (6 reworded, 2 wrong status); investigations: 9 of 49 fully correct, 24 more present (6 partial, 18 reworded). Errors are lead-in phrases ("this is probably ...") and test names ("allergy test" vs "Allergen skin prick test").
- **Actions / follow-up:** the plan is paraphrased into 1-2 items; gold decomposes it into advise-X, arrange-Y, start-Z, refer-W, follow-up.
- **Temporality / certainty:** 9 of 12 matched symptoms correct; no polarity flips (0). Too few matched facts to say more.

## 5. What the model does consistently well
- It produces a complete, mostly well-formed 18-section object in 25 (P01) and 23 (P00) of 30 records and keeps the section names exactly.
- Medication identification and doses (see above), vital-sign values when stated, assessment/diagnosis wording, investigation names, family and surgical history items (13 of 16 surgeries present in some form).
- It does not turn questions or possibilities into diagnoses in the cases checked; no polarity flips; no fabricated medication names.
- It keeps negation as a separate category (when it writes negatives, they are not turned into symptoms).

## 6. Terminology gaps to keep for the later fine-tuning phase
Chief-complaint normalisation (symptom wording -> clinical term), procedure names (gallbladder removal -> cholecystectomy, kidney function tests -> renal function test), vital-sign names, drug brand/generic aliases (seretide -> salmeterol/fluticasone, glycomet -> metformin, thyroxine -> levothyroxine), examination findings as short canonical phrases. These 190 present-but-unmatched facts are 17% of gold facts, as large as the 15.6% strict recall. They are better addressed by training or a validated vocabulary than by instruction.

## 7. Implications for P02
Every P02 rule must trace to one of rows 1-10 (traceability table in `P02_EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`). The largest single opportunity is not a clinical rule: it is stopping the evidence blob (rows 1-2), which fixes truncation, recovers whole records, and frees tokens for the clinical sections. The second is coverage definition (rows 3, 4, 7, 8). Rows 9-10 will not move much from prompting and should be reported separately from extraction quality.
