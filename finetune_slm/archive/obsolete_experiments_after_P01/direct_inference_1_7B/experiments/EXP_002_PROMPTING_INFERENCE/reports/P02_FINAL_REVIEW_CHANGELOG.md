## 1. Verification of the v1 template against the frozen schema (review item 2)
Programmatic check (`scripts/verify_p02_template.py`, results in `evaluations/p02_review/TEMPLATE_VERIFICATION_v1.json` and `_v2.json`). The frozen schema has key names and enums but **no types, nullability or required flags**, so type/nullability was checked against the 50 authorised development gold labels (`GOLD_CONVENTIONS.json`).
- **v1: 93 checks, 15 failed.** 7 were real mismatches; 8 were format-only (the values were present and correct but in a line format the checker cannot parse).
  - Real: (1) `symptoms[].duration.unit` and (2) `.normalized` shown as null placeholders inside an object that gold always fills when present; (3) `vital_signs[].value` shown as null but never null in gold (219 of 219); (4) `medical_history.surgical_history[].timeframe` shown as null, never null in gold (27/27); (5) `physical_examination[].body_site` shown as null, never null in gold (80/80); (6) the object shape of `clinical_actions.time_reference` ({value, normalized_date}) not described; (7) no statement that an empty string is never valid.
  - Format-only: allowed values for `negative_findings.{status,assertion,temporality}`, `review_of_systems.status`, `clinical_conditions.status`, `past_medical_history.status`, `social_history.{category,status}` were listed with shortened or mixed prefixes.
- **v2: 95 checks, 0 failed;** template fields: 130 of 133 OK, **2 documented deviations** (timeframe and body_site kept as `null`, see change 4), 1 note (`stated_by` constant `doctor`, gold 89/89). All 27 schema enums equal the prompt's value sets. Evidence conventions verified against gold: 1,772 of 1,772 evidence records are one exact transcript line; timestamps `MM:SS` and floors of the line times (4 end-time exceptions in 1,772); `supports` never empty; references symmetric in 50 of 50 labels.
- Unchanged and correct in v1: top-level key order, every category's keys and order, nested keys, constants.

## 4. Remaining limitations and uncertainties
1. The frozen schema cannot express a positive review-of-systems finding, a historical or resolved past condition, a non-oral route or the subject of a fact other than `family_history.relation`. The prompt avoids mislabelling; some information cannot be stored.
2. Nothing here shows the 1.7B model will follow a 3,910-token prompt; rules that were ignored in P01 (layout, role words) are the main risk and are measured by G1/G2.
3. The "check every section before finishing" step cannot be a real second pass.
4. Terminology gaps (190 of 1,093 gold facts present in other wording in P01) are not addressed by prompting and are a fine-tuning topic.
5. Thresholds are set on 8 hand-chosen development records; the labels are generator-derived. Pilot results are indicative of the gates only.
6. Memory margin is small; the longer prompt makes an out-of-memory failure more likely than for P01.
