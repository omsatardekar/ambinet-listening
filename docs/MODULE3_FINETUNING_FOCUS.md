# Module 3 fine-tuning: what to fix and what to cover

Status: 2026-11-19. Based on six hand-checked live runs plus the earlier EXP_008 study. Labels used below:
**[observed]** seen in outputs and checked against the dialogue; **[hypothesis]** a guess about the cause, not tested; **[proposed]** a suggestion, not decided.

Pattern behind F1, F2, F4, F5, F9, F20 [hypothesis]: with little to say, the 0.6B model repeats the most salient phrase of the conversation into every field it feels it must fill. It looks like repetition, not understanding.

Pattern behind F11 and the invented follow-up [hypothesis]: the frozen H1 prompt shows a schema template in which `follow_up` is pre-filled with `"type": "consultation"` and the uncertainty types are listed with `date_uncertain` first. The model may be copying template defaults: an invented follow-up consultation appeared in both runs where no visit was arranged (runs 3, 4 and 5), and the date uncertainty in every run. A short prompt without a template (planned for fine-tuning) would remove this.

**What works and must not regress** [observed]: numbers that are stated clearly are usually right (run 3: blood pressure, pulse and temperature; run 2: heart rate and blood pressure); explicit single facts are often right (run 5: blood pressure 148/92 and the knee replacement in 2019; simvastatin 20 at night, the bruises on the forearms and the shin, "heart trouble" as a condition, the blood-test plan, the temperature in run 1). Fine-tuning data should keep these easy cases, and the checks below should show no drop on them.

## 3. Problems that were on the display side, not the model [observed, fixed 2026-11-19]
* Correct vitals showed "No source found" because the transcript says "one twenty-four over seventy-nine" while the summary says 124/79: spoken numbers are now read as digits when linking sources.
* A correct allergy showed "No source found": extra fields such as `allergy_type` and placeholder values ("unknown") lowered the match score; they are now ignored.
* The same follow-up appeared twice in the plan; it is now merged.
* [observed, run 3] "No source found" appeared on exactly the invented items (the Rennie dose, "take clopidogrel before breakfast", the "52.48s" follow-up), because made-up values do not occur in the conversation and lower the match. **[proposed]** use "items with no matching passage" as one more automatic check on a model's output (it also flags some correct items, so report it as a rate, not as an error count).
* Note: the speech stage often already writes numbers as digits and merges consecutive turns of one speaker into one segment, so one linked passage can carry several facts.

## 3b. The SOAP writer (Module 4): what it did with these inputs [observed, encounter 54 and earlier]
* **Faithful.** Given the run-5 extraction it refused the invented items (the Tylenol allergy, "take lysinopril", the follow-up, the family history "dolores", the negative finding, the exam and review-of-systems entries, the chief complaint remark), turned Tylenol into an as-needed medicine, and wrote the water tablet as "name not stated". Earlier runs: it refused the invented allergy, vital sign, diagnosis and follow-up of runs 3 and 4 and recovered the real treatment (minoxidil) from the linked excerpts.
* **But incomplete when the extraction is.** The first note for encounter 54 had no tests in the plan although the doctor named a chest X-ray, an ECG and blood tests. Causes found: (1) a bug, now fixed: each linked excerpt was cut at 420 characters, so the sentence naming the chest X-ray was cut off and the writer correctly reported it as "not found"; excerpts now keep the sentences that mention the finding (650 characters); (2) a finding linked only to the doctor's question ("Do you live on your own?") gives the writer no answer; (3) what the extraction never captured (orthopnea, the 3-vs-10-day disagreement, SpO2 91 %, the exam findings, oxygen, IV treatment, admission rule) cannot be recovered from excerpts.
* **Measured on encounter 54, same agent, same extraction:** with the improved excerpts the plan gains the chest X-ray, the blood tests and the ECG and four vitals appear. With the whole conversation also given to the writer (`SOAP_INCLUDE_TRANSCRIPT=1`): six vitals including SpO2 91 % and weight, three exam findings (bibasal crackles, third heart sound, pitting oedema to the knees), three problems (heart-failure flare, irregular fast pulse, hypoxemia), a plan with tests, IV fluid-removal treatment, oxygen 2 L, the admission rule and return precautions, and an HPI with orthopnea, the nocturnal gasping, the duration disagreement and the chest pain denial. The two notes differ only in the input.
* **Implications for fine-tuning:** the quality of the SOAP note is bounded by the completeness of the extraction. A better extraction narrows the gap without changing Module 4. Giving the writer the whole conversation would close most of it, but the owner decided against it so that the extraction model carries the quality. Evaluate the final SOAP note, not only the extraction, with the judge and the checks below (the "Items to verify" list is long, 13 to 16 entries per note, mostly repeats of extraction errors; a better extraction shortens it).
* **Encounter 55 (run 6) showed a second consequence: false absences.** With the clinical summary only, the note had a blank Plan, "Physical examination: Not documented" and "No diagnosis stated by the clinician", although the doctor examined the hip, said wear and tear was most likely and set out a full plan. The writer could not know that, because the extraction's only plan items (an X-ray with no linked excerpt, "We'll see", an invented follow-up) were unsupported and it saw none of the doctor's real plan. With the whole conversation the note is correct and complete (right-hip X-ray, blood tests, paracetamol first, diclofenac only on bad days with food, walking stick on the left, exercises, "most consistent with wear-and-tear arthritis in the right hip", five vitals, family and social history, levetiracetam).
* **Decision (2026-11-20, owner): the SOAP step works from the extraction stage output only, not from the conversation** (`SOAP_INCLUDE_TRANSCRIPT` stays 0). Reason: if the writer also read the conversation, a strong LLM could override the extraction model and hide its weaknesses, which defeats the aim of making the extraction model strong. The blank Plan and the false absences above are therefore expected until the extraction is fine-tuned, and they are a measure of the extraction's completeness.
* **Small writer slips seen:** "Reports dieting" for a patient who said "Not really" (run 4); drug names normalised from a mis-transcribed spelling (the traceability check now accepts a close spelling).

## 4. What fine-tuning has to cover [proposed]

1. **Targets in full P02 JSON with a short prompt.** Stop prompt tuning (agreed earlier). Training targets use the same schema (arrays, never single objects) and **include `evidence_ids` with real segment ids**, so the model learns to cite and the text-similarity linking becomes a fallback. Keep the same 30 evaluation ids held out, scored by the frontier judge.
2. **Contrast data for each failure class** (examples where the wrong behaviour is tempting): denial lists with and without a present symptom nearby (F1, F8); a long answer that mentions one phrase many times (F2); medicines vs allergies, including supplements and "taken with water" (F3); a doctor who declines to diagnose, offering only possibilities (F5); a medicine the patient stopped, and one the doctor plans to start (F6); two sources giving different doses, an allergy that one speaker disputes (F7); a patient who plays the problem down, a family member answering for the patient (F9, F12); numbers spoken as words and as digits (F10); history spoken in passing (F13).
3. **Lists with different values per item** (several medicines with different doses and frequencies, several body sites) so the model learns not to copy values between entries (F16, F17); medicine names exactly as spoken, including unusual spellings (F17); units fixed per measurement (F18); advice such as "with food" versus "before breakfast" for different medicines (F19); a new prescription stated by the doctor near the end (F21); safety-net advice and planned tests spoken at the end (F22).
3c. **Absence benchmark.** Scenarios 04, 10, 11, 17, 18 and 19 have a `design.deliberately_absent` list in their `scenario.json` (for example: allergies, vital signs, diagnosis, follow-up, examination never discussed). Run 4 (scenario 04) filled all five of its absent categories. Report, for every model, the share of deliberately absent categories it fills (the aim is none), separately for allergies, because an invented allergy is the most harmful (F3, F23). Training data must contain many conversations where these sections are truly empty and the target is `[]`.
3b. **Empty is a valid answer.** Include sections that are genuinely empty so the model does not fill them (F2, F4, F5). Include `[]` where the conversation never touched the topic.
4. **Safety fields get extra weight** (allergies, negations, medication status, self-harm questions): either by upweighting these fields in the loss or by oversampling examples that contain them. Not tested.
5. **Conflict and uncertainty fields filled from the text**: the label set must name the real doubt (what value, which speakers), not a generic placeholder (F7, F11).
6. **Evaluation beyond the judge score.** Keep the judge, and add cheap automatic checks per failure class, reported separately for the old H1 baseline and each fine-tuned model:
   * F1: a symptom whose name also occurs in the negative findings.
   * F2: one phrase appearing as the main label in three or more sections.
   * F3: an allergy entry with no allergy/reaction cue in its cited or matched passage.
   * F23: any allergy, vital sign, assessment or follow-up entry whose words (and numbers) do not occur anywhere in the matched passages; any number in `vital_signs` that is a price, a time or a count of days, not a measurement (F24).
   * F25: a medicine name that occurs in the transcript only in a rejected or hypothetical context but appears with status "taking"; a topical product with route "oral".
   * F16/F17/F18: a medication field value (dose, frequency, route) that does not occur in the matched passage; the same dose and frequency on three or more entries; a unit that does not fit the measurement (oxygen not in %, pulse not per minute, temperature not in degrees).
   * F4/F12: `observed_by` = patient on an exam finding; assessment "stated by" the patient.
   * F5: an assessment while the matched doctor turns contain no diagnostic statement (needs a rule or judge).
   * F6: medication status "taking" where the passages say stopped/stop.
   * F8: self-harm or chest-pain question asked in the transcript with no matching negative or positive finding.
   * F10: numbers that occur in the transcript next to a vital-sign word but not in `vital_signs`; medicines named by the patient but absent.
   * F13/F15: schema-valid rate, list vs object, count of empty history sections.
   Also run the 20 scripted recordings in `scenarios/` (each `scenario.json` has a `design` block listing what is present, partial or deliberately absent) as a qualitative check.
7. **Order of work.** (a) turn the failure classes above into labelled examples and a checker script; (b) measure H1 on the 30 held-out ids with the checker, to get real frequencies; (c) train, re-measure per failure class; (d) re-run the scripted scenarios end to end through the speech stage.

## 5. Open decisions and facts to check
* **Which data may be used for training.** The Batch 001 corrected README says "Evaluation use only; do not use for fine-tuning". Check the READMEs of Batch 002 (ids 20001-20500) and of `batch_002_generation` (ids 501-1000, 393 draft files at the last check) before choosing the training set, and keep the 33 EXP_008 ids (30 evaluated + 3 spare) out of it.
* **Training environment** (hardware, method such as LoRA, time and cost) is not decided. The local machine has an Intel GPU and 2.7 to 4.6 GiB free RAM, which limits local training.
* **Real recordings need company approval** before use for training or testing; everything here is synthetic.
* The scripted recordings are generated speech: the speech stage and reconstruction add their own errors (merged turns, role labels), so judge the model on the transcript it actually received (`{NN}slm_input.json`), not on the script.
* The frequencies in section 1 come from two runs; step 4(b) replaces them with measured ones.
